## Long description

**ReviQuo turns plain WooCommerce reviews into video and photo social proof — and gives Divi a native module to display it anywhere.**

Text reviews all look the same. A customer holding your product on camera does not. ReviQuo adds webcam recording, video upload and photo upload to the review form your store already has, then lets you build reusable review displays and drop them into any Divi layout — product pages, home page, landing pages, footers.

### Collect richer reviews

* **In-browser webcam recording** — customers record straight from the product page, no app or file wrangling. Recording length is configurable up to 10 minutes.
* **Video and photo uploads** — accept an existing video file plus up to five customer photos per review.
* **Built on WooCommerce reviews** — no parallel review system, no data lock-in. Everything is a normal WooCommerce comment, and media is stored in your own media library.
* **Moderation-first** — reviews with media are held for approval by default, with an auto-approve option once you trust the flow.
* **Safe uploads** — anonymous submissions are restricted to approved extensions and MIME types, your site's upload limits and nonce-verified review requests.

### Display it anywhere in Divi

* **Native Divi 4 and Divi 5 modules** — not a shortcode wrapper. Both modules ship with a saved-display selector and a live, server-rendered preview inside the Visual Builder.
* **Reusable saved displays** — configure a display once in ReviQuo's Review Shortcode screen, then reuse it across modules, blocks, shortcodes and widgets. Change it in one place and every instance updates.
* **List, grid, masonry and slider layouts** — with independent desktop, tablet and mobile column counts, gap and corner radius. The slider adds optional autoplay, arrows, dots and swipe navigation.
* **Styling that matches your theme** — background, text, accent and star colours, plus toggles for the rating summary, filters, product name, avatars, review text, verified badge, photos and videos.
* **Pull from anywhere** — the current product, a hand-picked set of products or your whole catalogue, ordered by newest, oldest, highest or lowest rating.
* **Beyond Divi** — the same displays work through a Gutenberg block, an Elementor widget and a plain shortcode, so a theme change never strands your content.

### Convert more of the traffic you already have

* **Rating summary and review filters** — shoppers can see the rating breakdown and jump straight to the reviews that matter to them.
* **Verified-customer badges** — mark reviews from people who actually bought the product.
* **Media lightbox** — full-size videos and photos open in place, without leaving the page.
* **Rich snippets** — structured review data so star ratings can appear in search results.
* **Automated review reminders** — scheduled emails triggered by order status, with unlimited reminder rules, custom delay, subject and body.

### Built to live in a real store

* **WooCommerce HPOS compatible**, WordPress 6.8+, PHP 7.4+.
* **Fully translatable** — storefront buttons, recorder messages, filters, validation and admin strings all use the `reviquo` text domain, with a bundled `.pot`.
* **Privacy-ready** — registers personal-data exporters and erasers, ships Privacy Policy Guide text and cleans up plugin-owned media when a review is permanently deleted.
* **Modern React admin** — a settings dashboard that stays usable on narrow screens, not a wall of WordPress form tables.

### Every feature included, nothing phoning home

The Divi Marketplace edition unlocks every feature on day one. There is no separate license manager, no external updater, no advertising, no telemetry, no trials and no upgrade prompts. The plugin makes no outbound HTTP requests. Updates arrive through the Divi Marketplace update system you already use.

## Requirements

* WordPress 6.8 or later
* PHP 7.4 or later
* WooCommerce
* Divi Theme or Divi Builder for the Divi modules
* HTTPS for in-browser camera and microphone recording
