**ReviQuo turns plain WooCommerce reviews into video and photo social proof, and gives Divi a native module to show it anywhere.**

Text reviews all look the same. A customer holding your product on camera doesn't. ReviQuo adds webcam recording, video upload and photo upload to your store's existing review form. You then build reusable review displays in 13 layouts and drop them into any Divi layout: product pages, the home page, landing pages, footers.

### Collect richer reviews

* **In-browser webcam recording**: customers record straight from the product page, with a live preview and the option to re-record. They don't need an app or have to handle files. You can set the recording limit anywhere up to 10 minutes.
* **Video and photo uploads**: customers can attach an existing MP4, WebM or MKV video and up to five photos per review, with thumbnail previews before they submit.
* **Require the media you want**: you can make a recorded video, an uploaded video or at least one photo mandatory. The rule is checked in the browser and again on the server, so turning off JavaScript doesn't get around it.
* **Built on WooCommerce reviews**: there's no second review system and no lock-in. Every review is a normal WooCommerce review, and its media is stored in your own media library.
* **Moderation-first**: reviews with media are held for approval by default, and you get an email when a video review is waiting. Switch on auto-approve once you trust the flow.
* **Safe uploads**: anonymous uploads are limited to approved file types and your site's upload size limits, and only accepted from verified review form submissions.

### Display it anywhere in Divi

* **Native Divi 4 and Divi 5 modules**, not a shortcode wrapper. Pick a saved display or configure everything right in the module, then fine-tune fonts, stars, card borders and shadows, and Custom CSS from Divi's Design tab. A live, server-rendered preview appears inside the Visual Builder.
* **13 layouts**: list, grid, masonry, slider, media gallery, testimonial, spotlight, compact list, reels, marquee, timeline, accordion and chat bubbles.
* **Reusable saved displays**: set up a display once, then reuse it in Divi modules, Gutenberg blocks and shortcodes. Edit it in one place and every copy updates.
* **Responsive by design**: set desktop, tablet and mobile columns separately, plus card gap and corner radius. The marquee and reels animations stay still for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
* **Styling that matches your theme**: set background, text, accent and star colours. Toggle the rating summary, filters, product name, avatars, review text, verified badge, photos and videos on or off.
* **Pull from anywhere**: show reviews for the current product, a hand-picked set of products or your whole catalogue. Sort by newest, oldest, highest or lowest rating.
* **Beyond Divi**: the same displays work as a Gutenberg block, a plain shortcode and through a read-only REST API for headless storefronts, so changing theme never strands your content.

### Convert more of the traffic you already have

* **Rating summary and review filters**: shoppers see the star breakdown and can jump to a star rating or to reviews with photos and videos.
* **Verified-customer badges**: mark reviews from people who actually bought the product.
* **Media lightbox**: full-size videos and photos open in place, without leaving the page.
* **Rich snippets**: review photos and videos are added to WooCommerce's existing product data for search engines. No duplicate schema is created.
* **Automated review reminders**: scheduled emails triggered by order status, with unlimited reminder rules and your own delay, subject and body. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
* **Review reward coupons**: send a single-use WooCommerce coupon when a review is approved. You can set different rewards for text, photo and video reviews. Rewards never depend on the star rating, which keeps them within FTC and Google review policies.

### Built to live in a real store

* **WooCommerce HPOS compatible**, WordPress 6.8+, PHP 7.4+.
* **Brand-matched storefront buttons**: the record, video upload and photo upload buttons each get their own colours, icon, border, font, padding and size, with a live preview. The recording window is styled the same way.
* **Fully translatable**: storefront buttons, recorder messages, filters, validation messages and admin text are all translatable, and a `.pot` file is included.
* **Privacy-ready**: an optional consent checkbox, WordPress personal-data export and erase support, suggested Privacy Policy text, and cleanup of the plugin's media when a review is permanently deleted.
* **Modern React admin**: a settings dashboard that still works on narrow screens, not a wall of WordPress form tables.
