Setup instructions

1. Install and activate. Upload reviquo.zip under Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, then activate. WooCommerce must be active, and product reviews must be enabled under WooCommerce → Settings → Products → Reviews. Divi (theme or Divi Builder plugin) is needed for the Divi modules.

2. Turn on the review options. Go to ReviQuo → Review Settings → General. Choose which options appear in the product review form: webcam recording, video file upload, and photo upload. Set the maximum recording length (up to 10 minutes), mark any of them as required, and decide whether reviews with media are published immediately or held for moderation.

3. Choose storefront extras. In the Storefront tab, switch on the rating summary, review filters, verified-customer badge, rich snippets for search engines, and the optional consent checkbox.

4. Style the storefront. In the Appearance tab, adjust the colors, text, icons, and fonts of the record, video upload, and photo upload buttons and the verified badge. The Recording Modal tab styles the recording window and its buttons.

5. Set up review reminders. In ReviQuo → Review Settings → Email reminders, enable reminders and set the sender name and address. Add one or more rules: choose the order status that triggers the email (Completed by default), the number of days to wait, and the subject and message. Scheduled and sent reminders are listed under ReviQuo → Email Reminders.

6. Reward reviewers (optional). In the Review rewards tab, set up a single-use WooCommerce coupon that is emailed when a text, photo, or video review is approved.

7. Moderate reviews. New reviews with video or photos appear under ReviQuo → Reviews. Preview media in the lightbox, then approve, unapprove, mark as spam, or trash reviews individually or in bulk.

8. Display reviews anywhere in Divi (optional). Reviews with media show automatically on the product page. To place reviews elsewhere, add the Product Reviews module in the Divi 4 or Divi 5 Visual Builder. Either select a display saved under ReviQuo → Review Shortcode, or choose "Customize in this module" and configure the layout, source, and colors on the module itself. Saved displays also work with the [reviquo id="…"] shortcode and the Product Reviews block.
